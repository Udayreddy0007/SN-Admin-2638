     --> SLA : it is a time based contract / commitment between the service provider and end user (consumers)
         
           Service Level Agreement(Organisation and  End Users)

        
        ---> OLA :(Operational level aggrement)  -- it's a commitment between the organisation and the internal teams
               

               ex : 
                    When a user raised laptop  related incident 
                          
                          Organisation-SLA (4hr-for solution)
                                  |

                                    |
                                hardware replacement is done
