      Lifycycle of Incident : 
       

       Ex :  There is an employee not able to use his outlook   - Raise a Incident
       

      1 .  New  - The incident has been created here and  Haven't started Invistigation and Assigned yet
        
         ex : Incident is created with the details provided by the user and A number is generated 


      2 . In Progress - The Invistigation has Started and asssigned to a person who is going to fix the disurption
                        of the IT Service

        ex :  Now a Technian Start configuring the OutLook Accout and checking the connectivity
          
          --- From SN POV
            -- Invistigation Started 
            -- Check the issue since the incident is assigned to ServiceDesk group 
            -- One of the member (Technian) Start fixing the issue


      3 . On Hold - The Invistigation has paused Temporarly why cuz some required information dependency is nedded

        ex : Technian Temporarly stop Invistigation since he need some approvals / info about the issue from caller

        --- From SN POV

         -- Awaiting caller 
         -- Awaiting  Problem
         -- Awaiting  for Change  


      4 . Resolved - The Technian provides a solution to fix the disurption or Resolution

       ex :  Technian configuration the outlook account also fixed his connectivity

       ---- From SN POV
        
        -- Resolution code - How Incident  was Resolved
        -- Resolution notes - Solution how it is Resolved
        -- Resolved By- Who resolved the issue (Technian)
        -- Resolved On - Date and time of Resolution


     5 . Closed  - Technian will provide closure notes if  the incident completed the Resolution
      
      ex :  Technian  provide the closure information 
       
       From the SN POV - 
         Close Notes - How it is Closed

       
      6 .  Canceled  Technian cancel the incident since that incident has been resolved

       ex : By mistake the employee raised  duplicate incident . So  he raise a cancel request to cancel the Incident
         if there is no further use of that incident
